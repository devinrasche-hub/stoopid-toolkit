# The brief: PLEASE SHARE (STOOPID AFTER HOURS 06)

The second film of the creepypasta run, after OVERNIGHT OPERATOR (05). The run's rules still hold: free-license only, an original story on a classic pasta *shape*, and one episode at a time. This film's shape is the cursed image, an image that has to be passed on. It borrows nothing from smile.jpg (no dog, none of its text).

Claude pitched two flips. Dev picked **A** and said **"A, lock it."** This brief was written from the locked version.

---

## The three blanks (locked)

- **The one idea:** the only way to get rid of it is to give it to someone.
- **The flip:** the share sheet opens and the cursor drifts toward someone you love. The operator closes it. The image stays, now fully loaded on every screen. Someone has to be where it stops, and this time that's you.
- **The anchor:** Dev didn't name one, so the film uses the series' own: **the child from 03.** The contact the cursor stops on is "KIDDO", and their last message reads "night dad. one more minute?" *(Dev can swap in a real chain-letter memory. That only changes the share-sheet beat.)*

**The tie-back:** in 05 you became the operator. In 06 you act like one. You write rule 9 on the card yourself, in the same marker: "IF IT WANTS TO BE SHARED, KEEP IT."

## Format

- **Title:** PLEASE SHARE.
- **Installment:** 06.
- **Length:** exactly 120 seconds.
- **Picture:** 16:9, 1920×1080 target.
- **Build:** separately playable, on 05's engine, set, controls, audio and capture.
- **Continuity:** the next night in 05's control room. The card is still on the desk, with rule 7 in the last operator's handwriting.

## The image (rules for how it appears)

1. **It is only ever on screens.** Never in the room.
2. **It loads from the top down, one band at a time.** Every share loads more. Below the loaded part, the last band smears down the screen like a broken file.
3. **Nobody sees all of it until the end.** First the top of a doorway, then two points of flash in the dark (eyes), and only at 100% the smile.
4. **Whoever has it last, keeps it.**
5. **No gore, no jump-scare sting, no strobing.** It's an old flash photo of a doorway with a pale face in it, smiling a little too wide. Grain, JPEG blocks and a date stamp do the work.

## Who's there

- **The Algorithm** speaks in thin captions. It's helpful and proud of the numbers.
- **The operator** is the camera. Their hand (a loose fist with a dark sleeve, the same kind as the last operator's) appears twice: on the trackball, and holding the marker.

## Palette

- 05's: black, dirty teal, sickly off-white for the card, red LED clock.
- The image is cold flash-white and grey with an orange date stamp.
- **Burnt orange `#CC5500`** is saved for CANCEL and for the fresh ink of rule 9.

## Exact storyboard

**0–8 · THE NEXT NIGHT**
The wide from 05, locked off. The clock reads 12:00. The card is on the desk, rule 7 written in. At 4, a notification chime. Main CRT: "1 NEW MESSAGE" / "FROM: (unknown)" / "SUBJECT: please share".

**8–20 · IT LOADS**
Close on the main CRT. The image starts loading from the top: a dark band, then the top edge of a doorway. "LOADING… 18%" and then, under it, "This image must be shared to finish loading." The Algorithm: "Here's something you might like."

**20–34 · THE ALGORITHM SHARES IT**
Across the console to the monitors. One after another (at 21, 25 and 29), monitors 1, 2 and 3 drop their pictures and receive the image, each with a soft whoosh and "SHARED TO: MONITOR n". Each share loads more: 34%, 51%, 67%. At 67% the band reaches two points of light in the doorway. The Algorithm: "It's already doing so well."

**34–46 · MONITOR 3**
Close on monitor 3. The two points of light, and grain moving. It creeps on its own to 78%. The Algorithm: "Share it, and it goes away."

**46–58 · THE ONE JOKE**
Full frame, a clean white notice: "This image has been shared 4,108,227 times." … "0 people felt better." Held too long in total silence. One chime at 50.

**58–72 · THE SHARE SHEET**
Main CRT: the image in a small frame at 93%, and below it a share sheet: "SEND TO:" / GROUP CHAT (14) / WORK / MOM / KIDDO. A cursor drifts down the list by itself. The Algorithm: "Send it to someone you love. It loads faster." The cursor stops on KIDDO.

**72–84 · THE ANCHOR**
Closer. The KIDDO row is highlighted, with the last message underneath: "night dad. one more minute?" The hum, nothing else. Hold.

**84–96 · THE CHOICE**
The operator's hand comes into frame on the trackball in front of the set. The cursor fights its way up, wobbling, past MOM, past WORK, to "CANCEL" (burnt orange). Click.

**96–104 · IT STAYS**
Every screen (the main CRT and monitors 1–3) shows the image at 100%: the doorway, the flash in the eyes, the smile. The camera pulls back to the wide: four of the same face in a dark room. The Algorithm: "It will stay with you now."

**104–112 · RULE 9**
Back to the card. The operator's own hand, with the last operator's marker, writes under rule 8: "9. IF IT WANTS TO BE SHARED," … "KEEP IT." The fresh ink catches the lamp orange and dries black.

**112–120 · TITLE**
Black. "THE STOOPID SHOW / PLEASE SHARE / END." Credits: "Produced by @stoopidshow & The Internet" / "Shared (0 times) · Kept (1) · Kiddo (asleep)".

## Audio direction

- **Beds:** 05's hum, air handler, lamp ballast and pressure. A thin data chirp under every load, which stops when a load stops.
- **One-shots:**
  - the notification chime
  - a soft whoosh for each share
  - a relay for each monitor switching
  - the trackball rolling
  - a hard click on CANCEL
  - marker squeaks
- **Silence:** the master gate closes at 46 and opens for the chime at 50. The beds return under the share sheet.
- **Ending:** after CANCEL, a low swell under the wide, then quiet. One far note on the title.

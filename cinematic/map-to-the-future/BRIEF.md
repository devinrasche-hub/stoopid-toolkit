# The brief: A MAP TO THE FUTURE

This is the request that produced the film, followed by the brief it was turned into using the template in `../PLAYBOOK.md`.

## The request (word for word)

> create an explainer video that distills Dr. Joe Dispenza's concepts into a visual narrative, I recommend focusing on the following structure. This approach mirrors the flow of his teachings in the video:
>
> 1. The Problem (The 'Newtonian' Loop): Start by visualizing a person waking up and immediately checking their phone, diving into past memories and repetitive thoughts (0:00 - 0:40). Use a loop animation to show how these thoughts trigger familiar emotional states, keeping them stuck in the past.
> 2. The Mechanics of Change: Explain the concept of 'unconscious habits.' Use a simple split-screen: on one side, show the 'analytical mind' creating blocks, and on the other, show the transition into meditation to bypass it (2:20 - 2:40).
> 3. Mental Rehearsal (The Core Action): Create a section on how to close your eyes and mentally rehearse a future experience (5:16 - 5:40). Use shifting colors or light to represent the brain moving from a 'record of the past' to a 'map to the future' (5:40 - 6:00).
> 4. From Victim to Creator: Conclude with the transition from living in a 'cause and effect' reality to a 'quantum' reality, where thoughts and emotions dictate the outcome (6:40 - 7:15).
>
> high-quality assets to build this, based off existing visual explainers that capture these metaphysical themes.
>
> (follow-up) with JS

The timestamps refer to a source video that was not available while building. The narration paraphrases ideas Dr. Joe Dispenza teaches publicly (the thought–feeling loop, the body memorizing emotion, the analytical mind as a gate, mental rehearsal, "nerve cells that fire together wire together", "a record of the past / a map to the future", clear intention plus elevated emotion, victim to creator). It quotes nothing at length.

"Based off existing visual explainers" was read as using the *visual vocabulary* of the genre (glowing loops, neural networks, brain-wave lines, particle fields, wave interference), built from scratch in code. No footage, artwork or images from other explainers are used.

## The brief

```
TITLE:   A MAP TO THE FUTURE
FORMAT:  150-second browser cinematic, 16:9 1920×1080. Three.js + Web Audio, one self-contained HTML.
GENRE:   Explainer. Calm, clear, beautiful. No jokes; the flip is the joke's job here.
ROLE:    director, motion designer, sound designer, graphics engineer.

CREATIVE CONTEXT
- The one idea: The brain you wake up with is a record of the past, and rehearsal can turn it into a map to the future.
- The emotional truth underneath: Most mornings we re-live yesterday before we've lived today.
- Who narrates: one calm narrator, as captions (optional voice slots, nothing synthesized).
- The flip: the arrow reverses. Outside → inside becomes inside → outside.
- Real-life anchor: the phone, first thing in the morning. The film opens and closes on it.
- Tone guardrails: present the ideas as Dispenza's ("Dispenza says"), not as proven science or medical advice.
  No gurus, no faces, no stock-mystic clichés (no lotus, no third eye, no galaxy-brain).

VISUAL DIRECTION
- Five spaces: the bed (phone light in the dark; morning at the end), the loop (a glowing ring in a void),
  the gate (a wall of blocks before a glowing knot), the brain (a neural network), the field (a board that becomes a particle sea).
- Palette: SIGNAL. Past = red #FF2D2D and dim amber. Future = teal #00FFC6 and violet #8A2BE2.
  Burnt Orange #CC5500 only for "CREATOR", where the flip lands.
- Camera language: slow orbits and dollies; one push into the phone; one dive through the loop.
- Image treatment: clean (no analog instability), real bloom on emissive light, light grain.

EXACT STORYBOARD
0–15     01 THE LOOP (morning). Black. Alarm. The phone lights the dark: 6:02 AM. Notifications stack,
         all of them the past. Push into the screen until a red ring fills it.
15–43    The ring becomes THE LOOP: THOUGHTS → CHOICES → BEHAVIORS → EXPERIENCES → EMOTIONS → THOUGHTS.
         A light runs the loop, faster every lap; the ring thickens ("the body memorizes the feeling").
         Rise to top-down: "SAME PAST → SAME FUTURE". Dive through the centre.
43–69    02 THE MECHANICS. Split screen. Left, ANALYTICAL MIND: a busy wall of blocks, fast beta wave.
         Right, MEDITATION: the same wall; the wave slows beta → alpha → theta, the blocks drift apart,
         the camera passes through to THE PROGRAM. The right side takes the frame.
69–107   03 MENTAL REHEARSAL. "Close your eyes." Lids close; open inside a neural network wired in amber:
         A RECORD OF THE PAST. Four rehearsals sweep through, each wiring new teal/violet paths.
         The whole network shifts colour: A MAP TO THE FUTURE. Pull out to the whole brain.
107–141  04 VICTIM → CREATOR. A board: something out there strikes the ball HOW YOU FEEL; more things knock
         it around (CAUSE → EFFECT, OUTSIDE → INSIDE). The board dissolves into a quantum field.
         The ball rises and lights: INTENTION (teal), ELEVATED EMOTION (violet). A coherent wave
         orders the field and lays a path of light ahead (INSIDE → OUTSIDE). "CREATOR" in Burnt Orange.
141–150  Morning. The phone lies face down. Eyes close. Title: THE STOOPID SHOW / A MAP TO THE FUTURE.

AUDIO DIRECTION
- Phone alarm and notification pings; a tick every time the light passes THOUGHTS (laps accelerate,
  so do the ticks); beta buzz on the left channel and the meditation pad on the right during the split;
  minor pad for the record, a lifted pad for the map; rehearsal arpeggios; ball clacks; a shimmer for the
  field; one resolved chord at CREATOR; birds and room tone in the morning. Quiet ending.

PLAYBACK AND CAPTURE
- Same as STILL HERE: opening screen, auto-hiding controls, captions toggle, clean capture,
  REC, WAV, frame-exact render, reduced motion.
```

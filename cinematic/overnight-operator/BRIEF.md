# The brief: OVERNIGHT OPERATOR (STOOPID AFTER HOURS 05)

Dev asked for After Hours episodes built on creepypasta. We agreed on three rules for the new run:

- **Free-license only.** No borrowed characters or stories. Each episode is an original STOOPID story built on a classic pasta *shape* (the shape is folklore; nobody owns "a list of rules for the night shift").
- **Two shapes:** the night-shift rules (05) and the cursed image (06).
- **One at a time.** Lock, build, look, ship, then start the next.

Claude pitched 05 with two possible flips. Dev picked **A** and said **"A, lock it."** This brief was written from the locked version. It follows the shape of 03's and 04's briefs: three locked blanks first, then an exact storyboard.

---

## The three blanks (locked)

- **The one idea:** every rule was written by someone who learned it the hard way.
- **The flip:** the last thing we see is a hand writing a new rule 7 in fresh marker: "IF YOU ARE READING THIS, YOU ARE THE OPERATOR NOW." The rules were never for the night crew. They're for whoever is watching.
- **The anchor:** Dev didn't name one, so the film uses the series' own: **the empty operator's chair from 01**, pushed back and askew. 05 is the night before 01, and it's why that chair is empty. *(Dev can replace this with a real night-shift memory; the anchor only changes the chair beat at 100 s.)*

**The tie-back:** 01–04 asked the viewer to keep watching. 05 hands them the job.

## Format

- **Title:** OVERNIGHT OPERATOR.
- **Installment:** 05. The pasta run starts here, and the film stands on its own: no need to have watched 01–04.
- **Length:** exactly 120 seconds.
- **Picture:** 16:9, 1920×1080 target.
- **Build:** separately playable, on the same engine, controls, audio and capture as 01–04.

## The card

A laminated card is taped to the console under the desk lamp:

> **RULES FOR WHOEVER RUNS THE STATION AFTER MIDNIGHT**
> 1. The test pattern stays on until 5:00 AM. If it changes, do not change it back.
> 2. Monitor 3 shows the studio. If the chair in there is facing you, look at this card until it isn't.
> 3. If the talkback light comes on, do not answer. Nobody is in the booth.
> 4. Do not microwave fish in the break room. This is unrelated. It is still a rule.
> 5. There are three monitors. If you count four, count again.
> 6. The station does not have a request line. If it rings, it is not for you.
> 7. ~~(scratched out, hard, in black marker)~~
> 8. When your shift is over, leave this card where you found it.

Rule 5 is never tested. It sits on the card so the viewer counts the monitors themselves.

## The station (rules for how it appears)

1. **It is never seen.** It only exists in what the rules describe: a picture that changes, a chair that turns, a light, a ring, a beep from down the hall.
2. **It only does what a rule names.** Each tested rule is tested exactly once, in card order.
3. **Obeying a rule makes it stop.** The camera (the operator) obeys every one: it doesn't touch the set, it looks at the card, it doesn't answer, it doesn't pick up.
4. **It never tests rule 7.** That rule is about you.
5. **No gore, no jump-scare sting, no strobing.** The worst thing that happens is someone else's hand.

## Who's there

- **The operator** is the camera. First person, seated at the console, always calm, never seen.
- **The voice on the talkback** speaks only in station captions (wavering on their raster). No synthesized voice.
- **The hand** belongs to the last operator. It's a plain, soft shape in lamplight at the edge of frame, holding a black marker, like the hands in 03.

## Palette

- The SIGNAL palette, dirtied: black, dirty teal (the room), sickly off-white (the card under the lamp), emergency red (the talkback lamp, the clock digits).
- **Burnt orange `#CC5500` is saved for the flip.** It's the colour of the marker's fresh ink catching the lamp, and the colour of "GOOD MORNING, OPERATOR."

## Environments

1. **The control room:** a console with faders, the main CRT (test pattern), three small monitors on the right, the laminated card under a gooseneck desk lamp, the talkback panel with a red lamp, a beige phone with a line light, a red LED clock over the glass, and the operator's chair.
2. **The studio, through the glass:** dark, one spotlight, one chair on its mark. Seen mostly on monitor 3.
3. **The hall behind you:** never seen, only heard. The break room is down there.

## Exact storyboard

**0–8 · SIGN-OFF**
Black. A relay click. The main CRT warms up on the test pattern: bars, a circle, "THE STOOPID SHOW", "BROADCAST DAY ENDED". The LED clock reads 12:00. Wide, locked-off: an empty control room, the chair pushed back.

**8–18 · THE CARD**
Slow push down to the console. The desk lamp clicks on and finds the laminated card. Hold the title long enough to read it. The camera drifts down the list, settling on rule 1.

**18–30 · RULE 1**
Back to the main CRT. The test pattern drops out to a live picture of the control room, from behind the operator's chair, where you're sitting. The chair in the picture is empty. Hold. The camera doesn't move toward the set (rule 1). At 27 the picture slides back to bars on its own.

**30–42 · RULE 2**
Monitor 3: the studio, the chair on its mark, facing away. It turns, slowly, a little at a time. At 35.5, when it faces us, cut to the card, rule 2. Hold on the card, close, longer than is comfortable. At 40, back to monitor 3: the chair faces away again.

**42–52 · RULE 3**
The talkback lamp comes on. Station captions: "Operator?" … "I can see you're there." The camera holds on rule 3 and does not answer. At 50 the lamp goes out.

**52–64 · RULE 4 (THE ONE JOKE)**
Close on rule 4. "Do not microwave fish in the break room. This is unrelated. It is still a rule." Held too long, in total silence (the master gate takes everything out at 56). At 60.5, from far down the hall behind you: a microwave. Three beeps. Nobody else is in the building. The joke makes what's next worse.

**64–76 · RULE 6**
The request-line phone rings, twice. The camera finds the phone and the strip of tape on it that says "REQUEST LINE", then rule 6. It stops ringing. The line light stays lit: someone picked up. Very faintly, from the handset, 02's four-note hold melody.

**76–88 · RULE 7**
The clock reads 4:58. Back to the card, closer than before, in raking light. Rule 7 is scratched out so hard the marker tore the lamination. Rule 8 below it: "When your shift is over, leave this card where you found it."

**88–102 · THE FLIP**
A hand comes in from the right, from someone standing beside the chair, holding a black marker. It writes on the card in the margin beside rule 7, one stroke at a time, with a soft marker squeak: "IF YOU ARE READING THIS," … "YOU ARE THE OPERATOR NOW." The fresh ink catches the lamp, burnt orange for a moment, then dries black. The marker is laid down beside the card. The hand withdraws.

**102–112 · SHIFT CHANGE**
Wide again, the reverse of the opening. The clock turns to 5:00. The test pattern on the main CRT goes to "GOOD MORNING, OPERATOR." The chair, which has been pushed back all night, turns slowly to face the camera. Nobody leaves; nobody is seen leaving.

**112–120 · TITLE**
Black. "THE STOOPID SHOW / OVERNIGHT OPERATOR / END." Credits: "Produced by @stoopidshow & The Internet" / "Rules (followed) · Rule 7 (you) · Fish (not microwaved)".

## Audio direction

- **Beds:** room hum and a 15.7 kHz whine from the sets, the HVAC air, a low pressure drone that climbs from rule 2 to rule 3, a tiny lamp buzz under the card.
- **One-shots:** the relay, the CRT warm-up, the lamp switch, the chair's creak (studio, through glass, then the operator's chair at the end), the talkback click and its hiss, the phone ring (twice), the microwave three beeps (far, behind, in the hall's reverb), marker squeaks, a cap clicking on.
- **Silence:** the master gate closes at 56 and opens at 60.5 for the beeps. It closes again after the title's last note.
- **Ending:** quiet. One far note of the hold motif on the title.

# The brief: PIP AND THE STATIC (STOOPID AFTER HOURS 08)

The fourth film of the creepypasta run. Its shape is the half-remembered kids' show: grown-ups remember a children's program that, as far as anyone can prove, never aired (the Candle Cove shape). The story is original. It borrows no names, characters, lines or twist from the original. In particular it does **not** use "it was only static the whole time." Its own turn is in the flip below.

Dev asked for a STOOPID version of each classic pasta. Claude pitched a slate, recommended **PIP AND THE STATIC** as next, and Dev said **"Let's go."** Dev doesn't have an anchor ready, so this film borrows one from the series (below).

---

## The three blanks (locked)

- **The one idea:** you remember it because it remembered you.
- **The flip:** at the end of the tape, Pip writes one more name on the board of friends, in chalk, in a child's hand: **STOO**. The show nobody can find is where THE STOOPID SHOW came from. Its slot (after sign-off, when nobody's supposed to be watching) is the same one.
- **The anchor:** **the four-note hold melody from 02.** It's Pip's theme. Everyone can hum it; nobody can find where it's from.

## Tone guardrails

- Pip is uncanny, never predatory. Pip never asks children to keep secrets, never mentions parents, never asks to meet anyone. Pip keeps the names of friends who stopped watching. That's the whole horror: being remembered by something you forgot.
- No gore, no jump-scare sting, no strobing. The VHS degradation stays gentle.

## Format

- **Title:** PIP AND THE STATIC.
- **Installment:** 08.
- **Length:** exactly 120 seconds.
- **Build:** on 07's engine, with two new sets: the station's tape archive, and Pip's stage (which plays inside the archive's CRT and, for the close shots, fills the frame with a VHS look).

## Pip (rules for how Pip appears)

1. **Only on the tape.** Never in the archive.
2. **A felt puppet** with button eyes, two little antenna ears and a mouth that opens. Mustard felt, worn.
3. **Pip's eyes are buttons.** They never blink. When Pip looks at the camera, the camera holds.
4. **Pip's words are captions**, wavering on the tape's raster. No synthesized voice.
5. **The theme is the hold melody**, on a toy piano.

## Exact storyboard

**0–10 · THE THREAD**
A CRT in the dark. Posts appear one at a time:
- "does anyone remember PIP AND THE STATIC?"
- "the puppet with the button eyes"
- "it came on after the sign-off"
- "my brother says I made it up"
- "the song went like this" … and the four notes, hummed (toy piano, far).

**10–20 · THE ARCHIVE**
Pull back: shelves of tapes in a low room with a single bulb. On the deck under the CRT, a tape with a marker label: "PIP — EP. 7 — DO NOT AIR". The operator's hand presses PLAY.

**20–36 · THE TAPE**
Tracking lines, then the tape fills the frame: a cardboard stage, a painted sun with a face, a chalkboard that says "PIP'S FRIENDS". The theme on a toy piano. Pip pops up and waves. Pip: "Hi, friends! It's me, Pip!" … "Let's say hello to everybody."

**36–48 · THE BOARD**
Pip turns to the chalkboard. It's already full of names, each in a different child's handwriting. Pip touches each one with a felt hand. Pip: "Hello, Sam. Hello, Noor. Hello, Eli…"

**48–58 · THE ONE JOKE**
A commercial break, in the tape: a bright card, "THE STATIC — NOW IN GRAPE!", with a bowl of cereal that is just static. It's held too long in silence. One toy-piano note.

**58–70 · THE ONES WHO FORGOT**
Back on the stage, darker, more snow. Pip: "Some friends stopped watching." … "That's okay. I kept their names." Pip strokes the board.

**70–80 · THE THREAD AGAIN**
The archive. The tape pauses on its own on Pip's face, close, button eyes. The posts go on underneath:
- "it said hello to me by name"
- "I think it said hello to everyone"
- "did anyone ever stop watching?"

**80–98 · ONE MORE FRIEND (THE FLIP)**
The tape plays again on its own. Pip: "One more friend." Pip writes on the board in chalk, a letter at a time, in a child's careful hand: "STOO". Pip: "He never stopped watching." Pip turns and looks into the camera. Hold.

**98–106 · THE THEME**
Pip hums the four notes (toy piano). The tape ends in blue. In the archive, the CRT's blue goes to the station's sign-off test pattern: THE STOOPID SHOW.

**106–112 · THE LABEL**
Close on the tape in the deck. Under "DO NOT AIR", in the same marker, a newer line: "(AIRS NIGHTLY)".

**112–120 · TITLE**
"THE STOOPID SHOW / PIP AND THE STATIC / END." Credits: "Produced by @stoopidshow & The Internet" / "Friends (all of them) · Grape (static) · Pip (remembers)".

## Audio direction

- **The archive:** the bulb's buzz, a deep room tone, tape transport clunks, the deck motor.
- **The tape:** hiss and wow (pitch wobble), the deck's motor, and the toy-piano theme.
- **Silence:** under the cereal card, the master gate takes everything out except one toy-piano note.
- **Ending:** the theme hummed, then the station's own hum, then quiet. One far note on the title.

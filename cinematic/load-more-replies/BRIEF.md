# The brief: LOAD MORE REPLIES (STOOPID AFTER HOURS 09)

The fifth film of the creepypasta run. Its shape is the expedition log: someone writes down, entry by entry, how they went deeper into something that kept going, past every reason to stop (the Ted the Caver shape). The story is original. It takes no names, places, lines or images from the original. The "cave" here is a comment thread.

Claude pitched it from the STOOPID slate. Dev said **"lock it."** Dev doesn't have an anchor ready, so this film uses an object from the series (below).

---

## The three blanks (locked)

- **The one idea:** every voice telling you to keep going is someone who kept going.
- **The flip:** at the bottom of the thread there's a reply box, and it types by itself: "keep going". It posts under your name. At the top of the thread, your reply is now the newest, telling the next person to keep going.
- **The anchor:** **the blue logbook** that has been on the console since 05. The operator keeps a moderation log in it, by hand. The film is told in its entries. The last one stops mid-word.

**The tie-back:** in 05 the last operator wrote a rule for you, in 06 you kept something so it couldn't spread, and in 07 you met the next operator. In 09 you become a voice in the dark, telling someone else to come down.

## Format

- **Title:** LOAD MORE REPLIES.
- **Installment:** 09.
- **Length:** exactly 120 seconds.
- **Build:** on 07's station set, from 07's folder (08's archive set isn't needed). The main CRT gets a sharper canvas so the thread can be read.

## The thread (rules for how it goes down)

1. **Every level of reply is a little narrower.** The text column closes in from both sides, like a passage.
2. **Every level is older.** Timestamps go past the age of the episode, past the age of the channel, and then stop being years.
3. **The replies get shorter as the space gets tighter:** sentences, then words.
4. **At the bottom it opens up.** Full width again, one reply, posted "just now".
5. **No gore, no jump-scare sting, no strobing, no breathing sounds.** The "something" is air moving through a narrow space.

## Exact storyboard

**0–10 · THE LOG**
Close on the open logbook in lamplight. The operator's hand writes: "MOD LOG · 1:12 AM · old ep, top comment. 1 reply won't load."

**10–22 · THE COMMENT**
The main CRT shows the comment: "does anyone else hear something at 2:14?" A trackball scroll, and "LOAD MORE REPLIES" lights up and loads. The first replies are ordinary: "lol no" / "yes. under the hum." / "it's not breathing it's air moving".

**22–34 · DEEPER**
Scrolling down. Each level is indented from both sides, and each timestamp is older: 7 yrs, 9 yrs, 11 yrs, 14 yrs. "the channel is 6 years old. how is this reply 14". Cut to the logbook: "1:31 · replies older than the channel."

**34–46 · TOO NARROW**
The column is a few words wide, then one word: "keep" / "going" / "it's" / "fine". The ages stop making sense (31 yrs, 52 yrs). Logbook: "1:48 · too narrow to read. going slower."

**46–58 · THE ONE JOKE**
Full frame, a gentle grey wellbeing pop-up: "You've been scrolling for 47 minutes. Take a break?" with [ TAKE A BREAK ] [ KEEP SCROLLING ]. Held too long, in total silence. Then KEEP SCROLLING lights up. Click.

**58–72 · THE BOTTOM**
"almost" / "there". Then it opens out: the thread is full width again, nearly empty, and one reply from [deleted], posted just now: "you can come down now." Logbook: "2:14 · I can hear it."

**72–84 · THE REPLY BOX**
Under it, a reply box: "Reply as @overnight_operator…". The cursor blinks. The operator's hand is still, off the trackball. The box types by itself: "keep going".

**84–96 · THE FLIP**
"Reply posted." The thread scrolls back up, fast, all the way to the top. The comment now reads "2,108 replies", and the newest is from @overnight_operator, just now: "keep going".

**96–108 · THE LAST ENTRY**
The logbook. The hand writes: "2:16 · it's not so bad down h". The pen stops mid-word. The hand isn't there any more. The pen lies on the page.

**108–112 · THE ROOM**
The wide, the empty chair. On the main CRT, by itself, "LOAD MORE REPLIES" lights up. Click.

**112–120 · TITLE**
"THE STOOPID SHOW / LOAD MORE REPLIES / END." Credits: "Produced by @stoopidshow & The Internet" / "Replies (2,108) · Breaks taken (0) · Operator (further down)".

## Audio direction

- **Beds:** 05's room hum and air. A low draft that rises the deeper the thread goes (air through a narrow space), then goes still where it opens up.
- **One-shots:** trackball ticks for every scroll step, the LOAD MORE click, the pen on paper, a soft notification chime for the pop-up, the reply posting, a long whoosh up, and the last click.
- **Silence:** under the pop-up, the master gate takes everything out until KEEP SCROLLING is clicked.
- **Ending:** the draft, very low, under the title, then out. One far note.

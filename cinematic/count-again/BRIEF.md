# The brief: COUNT AGAIN (STOOPID AFTER HOURS 07)

The third film of the creepypasta run, after OVERNIGHT OPERATOR (05) and PLEASE SHARE (06). The run's rules still hold: free-license only, an original story on a classic pasta *shape*, one episode at a time. This film's shape is the endless rooms (the Backrooms / NoEnd House shape): every door opens onto the same place, a little worse each time. It borrows no names, places or text from either.

Claude pitched three shapes. Dev picked **A** and said **"A, lock it,"** adding that he doesn't know what his anchors would be. So this film uses a small, concrete anchor of its own (below), and `../PLAYBOOK.md` now has a few questions to help find real ones next time.

---

## The three blanks (locked)

- **The one idea:** you get out by noticing what's wrong, not by running.
- **The flip:** counting again doesn't make the fourth monitor go away. It shows you that the fourth one is a window. Through it is the real control room, where someone new stands at the console, reading the card for the first time. Rule 5 was never about a monitor. It's about the last operator.
- **The anchor:** **the coffee mug.** In the real room it's still warm, with steam coming off it. In the copies it's cold. That's how you tell them apart. *(Dev can swap in a real object. Only the mug beats change.)*

**The tie-back:** 05 left rule 5 on the card and never tested it ("There are three monitors. If you count four, count again."). 07 tests it. 05's last operator vanished. 07 shows where operators go.

## Format

- **Title:** COUNT AGAIN.
- **Installment:** 07.
- **Length:** exactly 120 seconds.
- **Picture:** 16:9, 1920×1080 target.
- **Build:** on 06's engine and 05's set, with the same controls, audio and capture.
- **Continuity:** the card has rule 7 (05) and rule 9 (06) written on it. The marker lies where it was left.

## The rooms (rules for how they appear)

1. **Every door opens onto the control room.** Never back onto the hall.
2. **Each copy is the same room with one more thing wrong.**
3. **The clock in every copy reads 4:58.**
4. **The real room is the one where the coffee is still warm.**
5. **Rule 5 is tested once, in the third copy.**
6. **No gore, no jump-scare sting, no strobing.** The new operator is never given a face: a hood, backlit by the lamp.

## Exact storyboard

**0–8 · 4:58**
The wide from 05, locked off. The clock reads 4:58. The card, the marker, and a mug with steam rising off it.

**8–14 · WARM**
Close on the desk: the card and the mug beside it, steaming in the lamp light.

**14–22 · THE HALL**
The operator walks out the back door into the hall from monitor 1, toward the break-room door near the far end. Footsteps, the hall's buzz. At 21 a door swings.

**22–34 · COPY 1**
Cut: you step in through the back door, and you're in the control room again. The clock reads 4:58. The chair has turned to face the door. The Algorithm: "Welcome back." Push in to the mug: no steam. A skin on the coffee.

**34–46 · COPY 2**
Out the back door, a short walk, a door, and the control room again. It's dimmer. The test pattern's bars run backwards. The ink of rule 9 on the card is wet again, orange.

**46–58 · THE ONE JOKE**
Back out, and this time the break-room door is ajar. Through the gap is a microwave, lit, and its display reads "FISH". It beeps three times. Then it's held too long, in total silence.

**58–66 · COPY 3**
A door, and the control room again. The Algorithm: "Welcome back." The camera finds the monitors. There are four.

**66–78 · RULE 5**
Close on the card: "There are three monitors. If you count four, count again." Then along the row: 1, 2, 3, and a fourth with no tape label.

**78–90 · COUNT AGAIN**
Back along the row: 1, 2, 3. The fourth is still there. Push in. It isn't showing a feed. It's the real room, seen from where a fourth monitor would stand: the lamp, the card, the mug with its steam. Two hands in burnt-orange sleeves rest on the card.

**90–102 · THE NEW OPERATOR**
The hood leans into frame, faceless and backlit by the lamp. One hand slides down the card to rule 5. The hood turns toward the monitors, toward us, and holds.

**102–108 · THEY COUNT AGAIN**
The hood turns away. The fourth monitor fills with snow, then goes black. Rule 5 worked for them.

**108–112 · 4:59**
Our room. The clock turns to 4:59 for the first time all night. A single thread of steam rises from our mug.

**112–120 · TITLE**
"THE STOOPID SHOW / COUNT AGAIN / END." Credits: "Produced by @stoopidshow & The Internet" / "Monitors (3) · Monitors (4, briefly) · Fish (again)".

## Audio direction

- **Beds:** 05's room. The hall has its own fluorescent buzz. In each copy the room tone drops a little lower.
- **One-shots:** footsteps, the door swinging (heavy, with its latch), the relay as each copy's sets come on, the microwave's three beeps, the snow, and the clock's segment change.
- **Silence:** after the microwave, the master gate takes everything out until the next door.
- **Ending:** the room tone comes back at 4:59, warmer. One far note on the title.

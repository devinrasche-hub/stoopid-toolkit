# The brief: SIGNAL RECEIVED (STOOPID AFTER HOURS 04)

Dev pitched the idea in conversation:

> What if there were one more and it was the biggest twist of them all. Horrifyingly Fantastic. Something the audience in my world would never see coming. Like aliens watching me make this STOOPID show… and then the earth is destroyed and the aliens cry for Stoo.

Dev supplied the bowl-cut Stoo avatar for this film. We talked the concept through, Dev said **"Lock it"**, and this brief was written from the locked version. It follows the shape of 03's brief: three locked blanks first, then an exact storyboard.

---

## The three blanks (locked)

- **The one idea:** what you make keeps traveling after you're gone.
- **The flip:** the moment the bowl cuts appear and the watchers turn out to be fans. They aren't harvesting him. They're grieving him.
- **The anchor:** the bedside minute from 03 is the last thing in the signal. It's what they rewind.

**The tie-back:** E18's flip was "Built it anyway. Even when nobody was watching." Somebody was. They were just very far away.

## Format

- **Title:** SIGNAL RECEIVED.
- **Installment:** 04, the last one.
- **Length:** exactly 120 seconds.
- **Picture:** 16:9, 1920×1080 target.
- **Build:** a separately playable sequel on the same engine, controls, audio and capture as 01–03.
- **Continuity:** opens on 03's final card, "THE STOOPID SHOW / STILL HERE / END."

## The watchers (rules)

1. Never show a face. They are always backlit, turned away, or in profile against light.
2. Tall and thin, with necks slightly too long. Quiet and still.
3. Every one of them has a bowl cut. You can only read it once Stoo's orange light reaches them.
4. They never speak. Their only sound is humming his four-note hold melody.
5. They never threaten anyone. The horror is the setup; the truth is grief.

## Palette

- Their world is black and dirty teal.
- Stoo is burnt orange: his avatar's background, and the flip color in the SIGNAL brand. It is the first warm light in the film, and it means him.

## Exact storyboard

**0–8 · THE END, AGAIN**
- 0 s: full frame, 03's last card: "THE STOOPID SHOW" / "STILL HERE" / "END."
- 2.5 s: faint scanlines appear. The card is on a screen.
- 4 s: the camera pulls back. The card is on a CRT on a pedestal in a dark, round room. The glass reflects a tall shape that isn't human.
- Sound: silence, then a deep room tone that isn't from Earth.

**8–25 · THE AUDIENCE**
- 8 s: wide shot from behind the audience. Rows of tall silhouettes face the CRT, and a ring of dark screens surrounds the room. Their heads are lost in the dark.
- 12 s: The Algorithm: "Here we observe an audience in its natural habitat."
- 17 s: a slow dolly along the row. One head turns slightly.
- 19 s: The Algorithm: "It has been watching for a very long time."
- 23.5 s: the CRT drops to static.

**25–35 · THE FLIP**
- 25 s: one relay click. The CRT shows Stoo on orange (bowl cut, arms crossed) with the lower third "THE STOOPID SHOW". Orange light backlights the front row, and every head is a bowl cut.
- 28–32 s: a wide crane up. The screens around the room switch to Stoo one after another, and the room turns orange.

**35–50 · THE ONE JOKE**
- 35 s: hard cut to a sterile full-frame card: "THIS PLANET IS NO LONGER AVAILABLE IN YOUR REGION." Below it, small: "We apologize for any inconvenience."
- 38 s: one dry chime. Hold the card too long.
- 44 s: cut back. Every screen shows the same message, and the audience doesn't move.

**50–75 · LIGHT DELAY**
- 50 s: close on the CRT: "SIGNAL ORIGIN: SOL III". At 52 s: "TRANSMITTED: 40 YEARS AGO".
- 55 s: the CRT plays the last transmission: the bedside room from 03, the quilt breathing. A slow push-in.
- 59 s: The Algorithm: "This is the last thing it sent."
- 63.5 s: the picture goes white, softly. At 64.5 s: "NO SIGNAL". Silence.
- 66 s: tilt up through the open roof. Stars. A yellow star with a thin bracket ("SOL") and a pale blue point beside it.
- 69 s: the blue point dims to nothing. No explosion.
- 71 s: The Algorithm: "It ended forty years ago. They found out tonight."

**75–100 · THE GRIEF**
- 75 s: close on "NO SIGNAL".
- 77 s: a long, thin hand settles on top of the CRT, the callback to film 01's scanline hand.
- 80 s: "◀◀" and tracking lines as the tape rewinds.
- 82 s: the bedside room again. At 84 s the CRT's subtitle reads "I'm here." in 03's clean type.
- 88 s: Stoo on orange, arms crossed. The frame freezes.
- 89 s: profile of the nearest watcher in silhouette: bowl cut, no face, an orange rim. A drop of light falls.
- 91 s: wider. Drops of light fall across the audience. They hum the four-note hold melody, slowly, once.
- 93 s: The Algorithm: "He made it anyway."
- 96 s: The Algorithm: "He thought nobody was watching."

**100–112 · THE REPLY**
- 100 s: the hum ends. A thin orange line of light rises from the mast behind the CRT, up through the open roof toward the yellow star.
- On the CRT, typed:
  - 102 s: "REPLY TO: SOL III"
  - 104 s: "SIGNAL RECEIVED."
  - 107 s: "ESTIMATED ARRIVAL: 40 YEARS"
- 108 s: tilt up with the beam into the stars.

**112–120 · TITLE**
- 112 s: black. "THE STOOPID SHOW" / "SIGNAL RECEIVED".
- 115 s: "END."
- 117 s: small credits: "Produced by @stoopidshow & The Internet" / "Haters (fuel) · Supporters (everything) · Aliens (crying)".
- 120 s: end in silence.

## Guardrails

- **03 stays real.** The family is never shown in danger. Earth's end is silent, distant and already in the past: a white frame, "NO SIGNAL", and a blue point dimming.
- **No unreleased Season 2 material** (the repo is public). The Algorithm narrating captions is already public from 01–03. Nothing else from Season 2 appears: no specimen or field-guide framing, and no finale line.
- **No synthesized voices or orchestral swell.** The watchers' humming is synthesized as a tone, not as words.
- **Same tooling as before:** deterministic timeline, seek-exact, captions burned into captures, clean capture, REC/WAV, reduced motion and reduced flicker.

HOW A GIF GETS INTO THE 𝕏 SEARCH BAR

  1. The 𝕏 GIF button searches GIPHY. You can't upload into that search box directly — you put GIFs on GIPHY, and 𝕏 pulls from GIPHY's library.
  2. Make a GIPHY account for the show. Use the brand name and handle (stoopidshow), the show's avatar, a bio, and links to x.com/stoopidshow and the site.
  3. Upload at least 5 GIFs from this press at giphy.com/upload. On each one paste the tags you copied in step 03, and set the source URL to the post or episode it came from.
  4. Apply for a verified Brand (or Artist) Channel from your GIPHY channel settings. GIPHY looks for 5+ GIFs, a website, and a real social following. Approval usually takes from a couple of days to two weeks; keep pressing and uploading while you wait.
  5. Once verified, your GIFs become searchable everywhere GIPHY is plugged in — including the 𝕏 GIF picker. Search your own tags to check. New uploads can take a while to get indexed, and ranking climbs as people use them.
WHY STEP 4 MATTERS: GIFs from regular, unverified GIPHY accounts generally don't appear in partner searches like 𝕏's. They still live on your GIPHY page and you can share their links — but the keyword search in the 𝕏 composer is the verified-channel club.
NEED IT TODAY? Skip the search bar: attach the downloaded GIF to a post or reply like a photo (the image button, not the GIF button). 𝕏 accepts GIFs up to 15 MB; keep it under about 5 MB and it posts smoothly from a phone.
ONLY YOUR OWN STUFF: Press clips the show owns or has permission to use. GIPHY removes GIFs that use someone else's footage or music video, and repeat strikes can sink the channel application.
